# Contributing to Flick

Flick is open source, and we welcome examples that show what it can create.

## What we accept

Submit a showcase example made with Flick. Add it under `examples/<your-example-name>/` with:

- the rendered animation or a link to it;
- a short `README.md` with a title and one-line description;
- the input type (`video`, `transcript`, or `script`) and aspect ratio;
- confirmation that you have permission to share every asset in the example.

Do not include private client material, secrets, or assets you do not have the right to distribute.

## Core maintenance

Creatorberry maintains Flick's core skills, scripts, starter project, and workflow. We are not accepting pull requests that change those areas at this time.

You are welcome to open an issue with a bug report or idea. If you change the core in your own fork, that fork remains yours to use under the repository license.
