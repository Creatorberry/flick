# Step 2: Write the Flick plan

Write `<output-directory>/flick-plan.md` before writing Remotion code. This is the user-facing creative contract. Keep it compact and do not put React, frame, or implementation details in it.

Use this structure:

```md
# Flick Plan

## Transcript
[The transcript Flick will animate. Include timestamps when available.]

## Format
[Approved aspect ratio and dimensions.]

## Brand assets
[Only user-supplied assets selected for this animation.]

## Creative direction
[The user's answer to “What do you think?” and Flick's concise interpretation.]

## Scenes

### Scene 1 — [approved scene name] — [transcript timestamp range]
- Transcript: [the exact line or lines]
- Animation: [what visibly happens]
- Sound effects: [only effects tied to visible events, or none]
```

Add every scene in this format. Split scenes from the transcript's natural beats. The transcript defines the spoken timing. Every scene needs a concrete visual animation, not generic text appearing on a background. Sound effects may support visible actions such as typing, clicks, impacts, reveals, counters, or transitions. Do not add music.

Show the whole plan and ask:

> Here are the scenes Flick will build from your transcript. Approve them, or tell me what to change.

Do not build until approved.
